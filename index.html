《军棋-海陆空四国大战》Android 隱私說明

營運者：LiTsunluROAD
聯絡信箱：lizunlu423@outlook.com

本說明涵蓋 Android 遊戲中的本機保存與聯機功能。Steam 帳號、TapTap 帳號及商店本身的服務，另受各平台說明約束。手機資料不與 Steam 檔案合併。

保存在裝置上的資料

遊戲會在本機保存設定、陣型與復盤。聯機服務也可能保存登入或訪客憑證，供下次使用及重連。清除應用程式資料會移除本機紀錄；解除安裝或清除資料後，不保證能恢復訪客身份、陣型或復盤。

使用 TapTap 個人檔案時，本機會依 TapTap 玩家識別分別保存暱稱、所選旗幟及已收到的房主對局結算，包括勝負、投降、離局、行動次數與可取得的殲敵數，用於顯示本機積分及統計。此檔案不提供雲端恢復，不與 Steam 合併；清除應用程式資料會移除它。

EOS 聯機

選擇 EOS 時，遊戲使用 Epic Online Services 的訪客身份、房間與 P2P 服務。服務會處理訪客識別、房間資訊、準備狀態、走棋指令及同步資料；建立網路連線也涉及 IP 位址等連線資訊。對局由房主主持，其他玩家收到遊玩所需的資料，連線可能直連或經過中繼。

相關資料處理請參閱 EOS 資料處理承諾及服務協議。

可選的 TapTap 聯機

選擇 TapTap 並同意相關說明後，遊戲才會呼叫 TapSDK 的登入及聯機初始化。遊戲請求公開個人資料權限以取得暱稱，並使用 SDK 提供的對戰玩家識別核對房間成員及訊息來源。房間、準備狀態、走棋及棋盤同步訊息會經 TapTap 服務傳送給對應玩家。

TapSDK 的啟動元件會保存應用環境並註冊本機畫面的生命週期回呼。TapTap 官方對登入服務另列有系統版本、裝置型號、CPU、網路類型、Android ID 與記憶體資訊。詳見 TapSDK 隱私政策及合規使用說明；國際 TapTap 帳號服務另見平台隱私政策。這些平台文件並不代表遊戲已開通所有地區或功能。

你可以取消 TapTap 登入，改用 EOS 或離線單人。TapTap 身份不會當成 Steam 身份，也不會用於寫入 Steam 戰績或成就。已由服務商保存的帳號或服務資料，需要依相應服務的方式管理。

權限與其他功能

此版本使用網際網路及網路狀態權限。套件允許確認指定 TapTap 用戶端是否安裝，以支援相關服務。遊戲未要求相機、麥克風、聯絡人或精確位置權限，也未接入廣告或內購 SDK。沒有要求綁定 Steam 帳號。

保存、管理與聯絡

本機資料由所在裝置保存；其他玩家保存的復盤屬於其裝置上的副本。帳號及第三方服務資料的保存、刪除或其他權利申請，需依服務商的適用說明處理。刪除本機資料不代表已刪除其他裝置或服務商持有的資料。

若你需要查詢、更正或刪除與本遊戲有關的資料，請聯絡上述信箱，說明問題與涉及的資料。請勿寄送密碼、驗證碼或不相關的個人資訊。我們會核對能處理的紀錄；由服務商管理的帳號資料，可能需要透過其管道提出申請。

年輕玩家與更新

年輕玩家應與家長或監護人一同閱讀本說明，並留意多人互動中的資訊分享。新增服務或改變資料用途時，會先更新說明及相應的使用流程。本說明不替代發行地區要求的實名、年齡或防沉迷接入。

Sea Land Air: Four Nations War — Android Privacy Notice

Operator: LiTsunluROAD
Contact: lizunlu423@outlook.com

This notice covers local storage and multiplayer in the Android game. Steam accounts, TapTap accounts and store services have their own platform notices. Mobile records are not merged with Steam profiles.

Records on your device

The game saves settings, formations and replays locally. Networking services may also save login or guest credentials for later use and reconnection. Clearing app data removes local records. We do not promise to restore guest identity, formations or replays after you uninstall the app or clear its data.

When you use a TapTap profile, this device stores your nickname, chosen flag and received host match results under your TapTap player identifier. Results include outcomes, surrender or departure, action counts and available kill counts, which are used for local scores and statistics. This profile has no cloud recovery and is separate from Steam. Clearing app data removes it.

EOS multiplayer

When you choose EOS, the game uses Epic Online Services guest identity, Lobbies and P2P. The service processes guest identifiers, room information, readiness, move commands and synchronization data. Network connections also involve information such as IP addresses. The host runs the match, and other players receive the data needed to play. Connections may be direct or relayed.

See the EOS data-handling commitment and service agreements.

Optional TapTap multiplayer

After you select TapTap and agree to the notice, the game calls the TapSDK login and networking initialization functions. It requests public-profile access to obtain your display name and uses the SDK battle player ID to verify room members and message senders. Room, readiness, move and board synchronization messages pass through TapTap services to the intended players.

TapSDK startup components retain application context and register local Activity lifecycle callbacks. TapTap also lists system version, device model, CPU, network type, Android ID and memory information for its login service. See the TapSDK privacy policy and integration guidance. International TapTap account services have a separate platform privacy policy. These documents do not mean that every region or feature is enabled in this game.

You can cancel TapTap login and use EOS or offline single player. A TapTap identity is not treated as a Steam identity and is not used to write Steam statistics or achievements. Account or service records already held by a provider must be managed through that service.

Permissions and other features

This build uses internet and network-state permissions. The package allows checks for specified TapTap client apps to support the service. The game does not request camera, microphone, contacts or precise-location permissions and has no advertising or billing SDK. Linking a Steam account is not required.

Storage, requests and contact

Local records stay on the device holding them. Replays saved by other players are copies on their devices. Retention, deletion and other requests concerning accounts or third-party service data follow the applicable provider's notice. Deleting local data does not delete copies on other devices or data held by providers.

For questions or requests to access, correct or delete game-related data, contact the email above and identify the records involved. Do not send passwords, verification codes or unrelated personal information. We will check which records we can handle. Provider-managed account records may require a request through the provider's own channels.

Young players and changes

Young players should read this notice with a parent or guardian and take care when sharing information in multiplayer. New services or changes in data use will be reflected in the notice and relevant user flows first. This notice does not replace regional identity, age or playtime-control requirements.
